# Views and edit mode

What each page shows, and in what order, is saved as data for your account. It survives reloads and works the same on every device you sign in on. You change it on the page itself.

## View templates

A view template is a starting layout. Pick one in **Settings → View template and start page** (or the first time you open Today):

| Template | For |
| --- | --- |
| **Morning and evening routine** | Your day in order: what fits now first, grouped by part of the day, in your own order |
| **Standard** | Everything on, sorted by time of day |
| **Minimal** | A plain checklist. Only what is due; done items fold away; extras hidden |
| **Gym-focused** | Training plans: a week strip and progress on Today, "done this week" on each habit, the Library sorted by most logged |
| **Quitter** | Breaking habits: Breaking first, quit counters and money saved, no Levels or prep |
| **Data nerd** | Every widget and line, consistency as the headline number, logged values in History |

After picking one, every setting can still be changed on its own. Your changes are listed under **Your own changes**, each with **Reset**. When you switch template, **Keep my own changes** carries them over.

Some ready-made habits suggest a view. The Habits page offers it once, with **Switch to …** or **No thanks**.

**Start page** sets which page opens from the app icon, a bookmark or the address bar.

## Filters and saved views

Today, Habits, Library and History share one filter bar: Areas, build or break, status, identity, habit, **Include archived**, sort and grouping (what each page offers differs).

- Sorts include **Time of day**, **My order**, **Name**, **Streak at risk first**, **Consistency**, **Newest first**, **Most logged** and **Last logged**.
- Grouping includes **Area**, **Part of day**, **Identity** and **Build / break**.
- **Save as view** names the current filter ("Morning"). Saved views show as buttons next to **Default**; delete one with its ×.
- Under **More options**: open the page with **What I used last**, **The page default** or a saved view; **Make this the default**; **Reset to default**. The Habits page also sets the **Lines on each habit** here (schedule, when, Area, consistency, last done).

## Edit mode

Pages edit in place. Press **Edit** (**Customize** on Stats) and the same page shows handles and controls; press it again to finish. Drag the ⠿ handle with a mouse, finger or pen, or focus it and use the arrow keys.

### Today

- Drag widgets, sections and habits into your order. Habit order is **My order**, shared with the Habits page.
- **Hide** or **Show** each widget (progress, week strip, streaks at risk, quit counters) and each section.
- **Done items**: **Stay where they are**, **Move to the bottom**, **Fold into a "Done" line** or **Hide them**.
- **Headline number** for all habits, or a different one per habit.
- **Only show what fits the current part of the day**. Today then says "Showing what fits Morning" with **Show all**.
- **Lines on each habit**: when and where, headline number, Minimum / Stretch buttons, reframe line, paired reward, reward jar, raise targets suggestion, identity votes.

### Habits

- Drag to reorder (this is **My order**, shared with Today).
- Tap a name to rename it.
- **Archive** or **Unarchive**.
- Drop a habit on a group or routine to put it inside; drag it left to take it out. With the keyboard, the right arrow puts a habit inside the one above and the left arrow takes it out. See [Groups and routines](groups-and-routines.md).

### Library

Tap a Task's name to rename it, and **Archive** Tasks in place. Archived Tasks keep their logs and leave the pickers.

### Stats

**Customize** on a board or a habit's Stats tab: add cards, drag them in the grid, resize them, edit or remove them from each card's bar. See [Stats](stats.md).

**Select** on the Habits page and the Library is the other mode of the same list: see [Bulk editing](bulk-editing.md).

## Switch features off

**Settings → Features** hides whole features app-wide: Identities, Levels, Rewards and paired treats, Cost and money saved, Prepare the day before, If-then plans, Reframe lines, Raise targets suggestions, and the Journal. Data is kept: only buttons and sections go away, and switching a feature back on brings everything back.

## Day and parts of the day

**Settings → Day and parts of the day**:

- **Day starts at** (default 04:00): logs before this time count for the day before.
- **Parts of your day**: named ranges such as Morning 05:00–11:00. They can't overlap. Habits can use a part instead of a time, and Today can group by them or show only what fits now. Removing a part a habit uses leaves that habit without a time or reminder until you pick another.

**Settings → Areas** manages your Areas (icon, name, colour). Deleting an Area never deletes the habits and Tasks in it.
