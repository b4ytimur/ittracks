# Views and edit mode

What each page shows, and in what order, is saved as data for your account. It survives reloads and works the same on every device you sign in on. You change it on the page itself.

## View templates

A view template is a starting layout. Pick one in **Settings → View template** (or the first time you open Today):

| Template | For |
| --- | --- |
| **Morning and evening routine** | Your day in order: what fits now first, grouped by part of the day, in your own order |
| **Standard** | Everything on, sorted by time of day |
| **Minimal** | A plain checklist. Only what is due; done items fold away; extras hidden |
| **Week first** | Plans: a week strip and progress on Today, "done this week" on each habit, the Library sorted by most logged |
| **Quitter** | Breaking habits: Breaking first, quit counters and money saved, no Levels or prep |
| **Data nerd** | Every line, consistency as the headline number, logged values in History |

After picking one, every setting can still be changed on its own. Your changes are listed under **Your own changes**, each with **Reset**. When you switch template, **Keep my own changes** carries them over.

Some ready-made habits suggest a view. The Habits page offers it once, with **Switch to …** or **No thanks**.

**Start page** sets which page opens from the app icon, a bookmark or the address bar.

## Filters and saved views

Today, Habits, Library and History each show their view as a sentence, such as "Default shows everything, by time, short rows with streaks, done at the end." Press a word to change it: Areas, build or break, status, identity, habit, archived, sort and grouping (what each page offers differs).

- Sorts include **Time of day**, **My order**, **Name**, **Streak at risk first**, **Consistency**, **Newest first**, **Most logged** and **Last logged**.
- Grouping includes **Area**, **Part of day**, **Identity** and **Build / break**.
- Each page has views: **Default** and the ones you make. The page is always on exactly one of them. The sentence starts with its name: press it for the views, the one you are on shown with a ✓, and press another to switch.
- **New view** (the dashed item after the views) makes a copy of the view you are on, and you type its name where it stands. On Today it can start from a template instead.
- A word you change changes the view you are on, and the line above the tabs says so, with **Undo** and **Keep as new view**. Every change is saved to that view as you make it, including the search and filters on the page itself.
- The view's own housekeeping is in the same menu, after **New view**, whatever the page is doing: on a view you made, **Rename view** opens its name where it stands, and **Delete view** removes it and puts the page back on **Default**, with Undo. On Today, while Default differs from how it came, **Reset view** puts it back, with Undo.
- The last item under the views says what the page opens with: **the view I was on last**, **Default** or one of your views. Press it for the next one: the line above the tabs says what the page opens with now, with Undo.
- On the Habits page, the sentence also says which lines each habit shows (schedule, time, Area, consistency, last done).

## Edit mode

Pages edit in place. Today, Habits and the Library have no Edit: everything changes where it stands, and each change says so in the line above the tabs, with **Undo**. The pages that still have a switch at the top (**Use** and **Edit**) turn into one you can edit with it; Esc goes back to Use. Wherever the order you see is yours, its rows carry the ⠿ handle: drag it with a mouse or pen, or hold it a moment with a finger and then drag (a swipe that starts on it still scrolls), or focus it and use the arrow keys. A tap on ⠿ (or Enter) opens the moves it can make: **Move up**, **Move down**, **Put inside** the row above and **Take out of** the row that holds it, where they apply. A layout change that can't be saved goes back, and the line above the tabs offers **Retry**.

### Today

Today has no Edit: everything changes where it stands, and each change says so in the line above the tabs, with **Undo**.

The sentence at the top of Today is its view: what it shows (everything, what is still to do, what fits now and so on), how it is sorted or grouped, how rows look, the number on a row and where done habits go. Press a word to change it.

- While the sentence reads **in my order**, each habit and tracker has its ⠿ grip, to drag, move with the arrow keys or tap for its moves. Habit order is **My order**, shared with the Habits page. In any other order there are no grips; pick **in my order** to bring them back.
- The last item of the "by" word puts Breaking first, or after the day.
- **›** after a habit's or tracker's name opens its row. An open row lists each kind of line under it (the Minimum, treats, reframe lines, jars and savings, identities, suggestions to raise) with **Hide** or **Show**, a hidden one struck. It belongs to the view, so each saved view has its own. When and where, and the number, follow the sentence's words for rows and the number.
- **Number** in an open row gives that habit a number of its own instead of the view's.

Sections and groups fold, and stay folded. Every count on Today (the count under the title, each section, the week strip) counts the habits themselves: a group or routine counts through the habits inside it. A group that is done by its rule while a habit inside is still due stays with what is still to do, so that habit isn't filed under Done.

### Habits

Habits has no Edit. Its one other state is **Select**, in the title line: see [Bulk editing](bulk-editing.md).

- While the sentence's order reads **in my order**, each habit has its ⠿ grip (this is **My order**, shared with Today). Sorted by name, by consistency or newest first there are no grips; pick **in my order** to bring them back. A habit's **Inside** line on its own page moves it into a group or routine in any order.
- Up and down picks the place; sideways picks how deep. To the right puts a habit inside the group or routine above it, to the left takes it out; the line's start says which. With the keyboard, the right arrow puts a habit inside the one above and the left arrow takes it out. See [Groups and routines](groups-and-routines.md).
- A habit's **⋯** holds **Rename**, which opens its name as a box where it stands, and **Archive** (**Unarchive**, or **Put back** for a deleted one). Archive acts at once: the habit leaves the list, and the line above the tabs has the Undo.

### Library

The Library has no Edit either. A Task's **⋯** holds **Rename** and **Archive**, as on Habits. Archived Tasks keep their logs and leave the pickers. Tasks have no order of their own, so they have no grips. **Select** changes several at once.

### Areas

Group Habits or the Library by Area. Each Area's heading has its ⠿ grip, to reorder the Areas, and a **⋯** with **Rename** (its name opens where it stands), **Colour and icon**, and **Delete** (with Undo; nothing in it is deleted). While nothing filters the list, empty Areas show too, and **New Area** at the end adds one.

Changing a habit's Area counts from today on, like moving it into a group: Stats and History keep its past days in the Area they had, so moving a chore from one kid to another doesn't move the credit for what was done before. The band offers **Past days too** to put every day in the new Area, with an Undo. A bulk Area change also counts from today on.

### Stats

Stats has no Edit. Every card shows its ⠿, a board's tab drags to move the board, and the ⋯ beside the tabs holds **Rename this board**, **Move…** and **Delete board**. What a card shows is changed in its own words. See [Stats](stats.md).

**Select** on the Habits page and the Library is their one other state: a tap ticks a row instead of opening it. See [Bulk editing](bulk-editing.md).

## Switch features off

**Settings → Features** hides whole features app-wide: Identities, Levels, Rewards and paired treats, Cost and money saved, Prepare the day before, If-then plans, Reframe lines, Raise targets suggestions, and the Journal. Data is kept: only buttons and sections go away, and switching a feature back on brings everything back.

## Day and parts of the day

**Settings → Day starts** and **Settings → Parts of the day**:

- **Day starts at** (default 04:00): logs before this time count for the day before.
- **Parts of the day**: named ranges such as Morning 05:00–11:00. They can't overlap. Habits can use a part instead of a time, and Today can group by them or show only what fits now. Removing a part a habit uses leaves that habit without a time or reminder until you pick another.
