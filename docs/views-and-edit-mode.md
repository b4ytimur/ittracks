# Views and edit mode

What each page shows, and in what order, is saved as data for your account. It survives reloads and works the same on every device you sign in on. You change it on the page itself.

## View templates

A view template is a starting layout. Pick one in **Settings → View template** (or the first time you open Today):

| Template | For |
| --- | --- |
| **Morning and evening routine** | Your day in order: what fits now first, grouped by part of the day, in your own order |
| **Standard** | Everything on, sorted by time of day |
| **Minimal** | A plain checklist. Only what is due; done items fold away; extras hidden |
| **Week first** | Plans: a week strip and progress on Today, "done this week" on each habit, the Library sorted by most logged |
| **Quitter** | Breaking habits: Breaking first, quit counters and money saved, no Levels or prep |
| **Data nerd** | Every widget and line, consistency as the headline number, logged values in History |

After picking one, every setting can still be changed on its own. Your changes are listed under **Your own changes**, each with **Reset**. When you switch template, **Keep my own changes** carries them over.

Some ready-made habits suggest a view. The Habits page offers it once, with **Switch to …** or **No thanks**.

**Start page** sets which page opens from the app icon, a bookmark or the address bar.

## Filters and saved views

Today, Habits, Library and History share one filter bar: Areas, build or break, status, identity, habit, **Include archived**, sort and grouping (what each page offers differs).

- Sorts include **Time of day**, **My order**, **Name**, **Streak at risk first**, **Consistency**, **Newest first**, **Most logged** and **Last logged**.
- Grouping includes **Area**, **Part of day**, **Identity** and **Build / break**.
- Each page has views: **Default** and the ones you make. The page is always on exactly one of them, shown with a ✓. Press another to switch.
- **+ New view** (the dashed button after the views) makes one: type its name and press Enter. It starts as a copy of the view you were on, and **Customize** opens on it.
- **Customize** changes the view you are on, and says which: "Changes save to Morning". Every change is saved to that view as you make it, including the search and filters on the page itself and a drag that switches the sort to **My order**. **Delete view** removes it and puts the page back on **Default**.
- Under **More options**: open the page with **The view I was on last**, **Default** or one of your views. The Habits page also sets the **Lines on each habit** here (schedule, when, Area, consistency, last done).

## Edit mode

Pages edit in place. The switch at the top of a page (**Use**, **Edit**, and **Select** where there is something to change together) turns the same list into one you can edit; Esc goes back to Use. Drag the ⠿ handle with a mouse, finger or pen, or focus it and use the arrow keys. A layout change that can't be saved goes back, and the line above the tabs offers **Retry**.

### Today

- Drag widgets, sections and habits into your order. Habit order is **My order**, shared with the Habits page.
- **Hide** or **Show** each widget (progress, week strip, streaks at risk, quit counters) and each section.
- **Layout**: **Outline**, **Up next first**, **Area blocks** or **One list**. It belongs to the view, so each saved view has its own. **Reset layout** puts back the template's, with Undo.
- Sections and groups fold, and stay folded.
- Under **Customize → More options**: what happens to done items, the **Headline number** (a habit can have its own, in Edit), **Only show what fits the current part of the day**, and the **Lines on each habit**.

### Habits

- Drag to reorder (this is **My order**, shared with Today).
- Tap a name to rename it.
- **Archive** strikes a habit where it stands; **Unarchive** takes that back. When you leave Edit it goes, and the line above the tabs has the Undo.
- Up and down picks the place; sideways picks how deep. To the right puts a habit inside the group or routine above it, to the left takes it out; the line's start says which. With the keyboard, the right arrow puts a habit inside the one above and the left arrow takes it out. See [Groups and routines](groups-and-routines.md).

### Library

Tap a Task's name to rename it, and **Archive** Tasks in place. Archived Tasks keep their logs and leave the pickers.

### Areas

Group Habits or the Library by Area, then choose **Edit**: drag an Area's heading to reorder, tap its name to rename it, press its dot for its colour and icon, **Delete** it (with Undo; nothing in it is deleted), or add one at the end.

### Stats

Drag cards, and rename the board. What a card shows is changed in its own words, in Use too. See [Stats](stats.md).

**Select** on the Habits page and the Library is the other mode of the same list: see [Bulk editing](bulk-editing.md).

## Switch features off

**Settings → Features** hides whole features app-wide: Identities, Levels, Rewards and paired treats, Cost and money saved, Prepare the day before, If-then plans, Reframe lines, Raise targets suggestions, and the Journal. Data is kept: only buttons and sections go away, and switching a feature back on brings everything back.

## Day and parts of the day

**Settings → Day starts** and **Settings → Parts of the day**:

- **Day starts at** (default 04:00): logs before this time count for the day before.
- **Parts of the day**: named ranges such as Morning 05:00–11:00. They can't overlap. Habits can use a part instead of a time, and Today can group by them or show only what fits now. Removing a part a habit uses leaves that habit without a time or reminder until you pick another.
